Song rarity

Know when the room got lucky.

Eligible songs are grouped into five rarity tiers based on their live history.

Ultra RareThe rarest live appearances.
RareA serious deep cut.
UncommonNot an everyday setlist find.
RegularPlayed with some frequency.
StapleA regular part of the live catalog.

New is a separate designation for a song at the beginning of its live history.

Curated, not claimed perfect.

Red Mosquito combines and reviews information from multiple public sources. Live music history is full of alternate titles, tags, venue aliases and disputed details; corrections are welcomed and credited in the app’s data updates.
